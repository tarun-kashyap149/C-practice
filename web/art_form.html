Art's of India

Lets Explore some famous arts of India.  Thousents of people come India to explore them.

Dances of India

Classical Dances:

  1. Bharatanatyam - Tamil Nadu, oldest classical dance, full of expressions and footwork
  2. Kathak-Uttar Pradesh, famous for fast spins and foot tapping
  3. Kathakali - Kerala, dancers wear heavy makeup and costumes, tell stories from Ramayana/Mahabharata
  4. Odissi - Odisha, graceful and soft movements
  5. Kuchipudi - Andhra Pradesh, mix of dance and drama
  6. Manipuri - Manipur, very calm and devotional, based on Radha-Krishna
  7. Mohiniyattam - Kerala, only performed by women, very graceful
  8. Sattriya - Assam, from monasteries of Assam

Painting art's of India

Painting Arts:

  1. Madhubani Painting - Bihar, made with fingers and sticks, bright colours, stories of gods.
  2. Warli Painting - Maharashtra, white colour on mud wall, triangle humans.
  3. Pattachitra - Odisha, painting on cloth, Jagannath stories.
  4. Miniature Painting - Rajasthan, very small and detailed paintings of kings.

Handicraft art's of India

Handicraft Arts:

  1. Blue Pottery - Jaipur, blue colour pottery
  2. Terracotta - West Bengal, clay art
  3. Chikankari - Lucknow, beautiful hand embroidery on clothes
  4. Bandhani - Gujarat / Rajasthan, tie and dye art
